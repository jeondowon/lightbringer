Edits: trim leading silence, mono conversion, fixed-speed resampling on selected layers,
low-pass filtering, layering, occasional reverse texture, fades, conservative peak normalization.
The exact per-cue recipe and source mapping are in Assets/Scripts/Editor/FoleySoundBake.cs.
Rebuild in Unity: Lightbringer > Audio > Rebuild Foley Sound Effects.
No external audio tool, Windows API or network is needed to rebuild from the bundled sources.

Runtime: Esc / P > 효과음 controls a separate saved SFX volume (default 70%).
20 world voices and 4 UI voices; distance attenuation, repetition limits, priorities.
Pause/level-up suspend battlefield voices while interface feedback remains available.
Common attacks choose multiple takes with subtle +/-2% pitch variation.

Verify: Lightbringer > Validate Campaign in Play Mode.
Results: Docs/Validation/CampaignPlayChecks.txt and Docs/Validation/FoleyBakeChecks.txt.
Automated validation covers audio data and playback behavior, not subjective sound quality.

Shared SFX level and full slider range (2026-10-13):
  All 20 baked WAVs now share a maximum sliding 100ms RMS of -20 dBFS (+/- 0.1 dB).
  This is unweighted short-window RMS, not integrated LUFS or whole-file average.
  Whole-file RMS remains different because silence and decay lengths differ.
  The offline bake measures after edge fades and limits sample peaks to -1 dBFS.
  Only transients exceeding the ceiling use a 1ms look-ahead / 10ms release gain envelope.
  Source files, selections (including the second arrow hit), durations and pitch remain unchanged.
  The old per-cue runtime gain of 0.16-0.42 is removed; AudioSource volume follows the user's
    existing SFX slider directly. Saved preferences and default 70% are retained.
  At default 70%, each clip's maximum 100ms RMS is about -23.10 dBFS before spatial attenuation.
  World distance falloff and voice/rate limits remain; the per-file ceiling is not a final mix limiter.
  All individual imports and the full rebuild automatically apply the same normalization.
  Rebuild and checks: Lightbringer > Audio > Rebuild and Validate Normalized Sound Effects.
  Before/after measurements: Docs/Audio/SoundLevelsBeforeNormalization.csv and SoundLevels.csv.
