Lightbringer orchestral demo

Original composition and arrangement for this project.
All audible instruments use acoustic recordings from VSCO 2 Community Edition.
No paid APIs, subscriptions, synthetic oscillator instruments, or electronic drum sounds.

Sample library: VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition

OrchestralSamples.json records each source path, size, and original Git blob hash.

Recreate with PowerShell:
  ./Get-OrchestralSamples.ps1  (one-time free sample download)
  ./New-OrchestralDemo.ps1     (offline render)

Instrumentation:
  Trumpet, violin section, flute; cello, acoustic bass drum, snare and cymbal.
  128 BPM, D minor, 32 bars, 60 seconds, circular reverb tails for looping.
  0-15s trumpet theme; 15-30s violin; 30-45s flute; 45-60s ensemble reprise.
The source recordings use C3 for MIDI 60; the manifest maps this octave convention.
Previous synthetic demos are retained as separate files.
